## Recipe 1 (savory)

* avocado oil 
  * 2 tbsp 
* tigernut powder 
  * 15 g 
* bone broth powder 
  * 22 g 
* greens powder 
  * 3g 
* beef organs powder 
  * 1 capsule 
* spices? 
  * with no spices the flavor is very mild/neutral 
  * flavor can be added via spices e.g. ½ tsp of chili powder, salt to taste 
* 400 mL water

calories: 412 
carbs: 12g 
fibre: 3g 
price: 2.83CAD

## Recipe 2 (sweet)

* tigernut powder 
  * 12g 
* egg white protein powder or whey protein isolate (unflavored) 
  * 12g 
* light olive oil or avocado oil 
  * 1 tsp 
* carob powder 
  * 6g 
* monkfruit/date powder to taste 
* multivitamin powder 
  * 1 capsule 
* fruit powder 
  * 6g 
* 400 mL water

## Tools

* Extra-large (32oz+) shaker bottle for double servings (use two whisk balls)
* Blender

Either of these can be cleaned by shaking/blending with water and detergent. Some blenders have a self-clean mode that uses heat and pulses. A dishwasher can also be used.

Use extra-long straws for hands-free drinking.
